Why VEFS Foundation?

Organic Farming & Skill Training

  • Promoting organic and natural farming rooted in traditional wisdom
  • Seed saving focused on native and indigenous varieties
  • Skill development for sustainable cultivation techniques
  • Workshops on natural fertilizers and soil regeneration
  • Eco-friendly pest management solutions
  • Empowering farmers towards self-reliant agriculture

Biodiversity Activities

  • Lake restoration to revive natural water ecosystems
  • Afforestation with native and indigenous tree species
  • Habitat restoration for birds, insects, and wildlife
  • Community ecological conservation programs
  • Awareness activities to protect local biodiversity
  • Long-term environmental regeneration efforts

Social Welfare Activities

  • Awareness on healthy and sustainable food choices
  • Good Food movement promoting chemical-free agriculture
  • Educating about harmful pesticide usage
  • Self-sustainable and mindful living practices
  • Healthier lifestyles through natural food awareness
  • Building conscious communities for a sustainable future

Kids Activities

  • Fun-filled workshops on farming and ecology
  • Hands-on learning with eco-friendly products
  • Creative sessions using recycled and natural materials
  • Natural color preparation for artistic exploration
  • Handmade craft activities inspired by nature
  • Environmental awareness through play and creativity

Seed Banks

  • Preservation of native and indigenous seed varieties
  • Community seed sharing for farmers and home growers
  • Promoting seed sovereignty and biodiversity conservation
  • Multiplication of traditional seed varieties
  • Supporting self-sustainable farming communities
  • Protecting agricultural heritage for future generations

Nature Agro Tourism

  • Curated visits to natural and organic farms
  • Learn directly from farmers with sustainable techniques
  • Guided forest walks exploring native tree species
  • Immersive experiences to reconnect with nature
  • Educational eco-tours blending learning and celebration
  • Experience farming, food, and forest naturally
