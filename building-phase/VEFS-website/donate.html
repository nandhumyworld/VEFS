Donation FAQs

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! VEFS Foundation is registered under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. You'll receive an 80G certificate for tax exemption. Donations are eligible for 50% tax deduction under Indian tax laws.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. Donations made via bank transfer go directly to VEFS Foundation's HDFC Bank account. UPI payments are processed securely through your own UPI app. We do not store any financial data.

Can I set up a regular donation?

Yes! If you'd like to set up a recurring contribution, please contact us at vefsfoundation@gmail.com and we'll help arrange it.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept bank transfers (HDFC Bank) and UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and all UPI apps). Please use the account details or UPI ID shown at the top of this page.

Will I receive a receipt?

Yes — after you submit the form on this page, we'll send a confirmation email with your receipt. If you requested an 80G certificate, we'll follow up for your PAN number.

Can I donate on behalf of someone else?

Yes! You can make a donation in honor or memory of someone. Simply mention this in the message field when filling the form, or contact us to arrange a personalized acknowledgment.
